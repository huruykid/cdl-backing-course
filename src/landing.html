BEFORE YOU CLIMB IN

A few things to know.

Do I need to know how to drive a truck?

No. The guided first drive starts with the controls and introduces how the tractor and trailer move together. Experienced players can go directly to the drills.

Can I play on my phone?

Yes. Hold the on-screen pedals and drag the steering wheel. On a keyboard, use the arrow keys to drive and steer. The setup flow shows you the controls before you start.

Does this use official CDL test scoring?

No. This is a practice game with its own dimensions, physics and scoring. It is not an official test simulator or a substitute for instruction and practice in a real vehicle.

Will my progress be saved?

Your completed drills, best stars and preferences are stored in this browser when local storage is available. They do not sync between devices. Clearing browser data removes them.
